hijackscan zone report - 100 .ee domains

Risk of certificate theft through BGP hijacks below DNS (CVSS severity scale), and what each fix closes

How to read this report.

The 100 domains are a seeded random sample (seed 1) of the 204,698 delegations in the .ee zone file (SOA serial 1793464230). Each of the 100 domains was looked up once (web and nameserver addresses, CAA, DS, DNSKEY with the AD flag) and every address was matched against BGP (announced prefix, origin AS) and the RPKI ROAs of 2026-10-31. Each domain then gets a risk score from 0 to 10: the chance that the best attack path yields a certificate within 5 requests, under the CA/B Forum multi-perspective rule (4 remote perspectives, 1 may disagree), discounted by 0.87 per high-complexity step (the CVSS 3.1 AC:H / AC:L ratio). Severity uses the CVSS rating scale. Derivation and references: design/formula.md. No domain names appear below.

The whole .ee zone

What the zone file says for free.

The parent zone lists every delegation and which of them have a DS record. That is the population the sample estimates. DS at parent is an upper bound on DNSSEC that validates: zones signed without a DS (islands) are not counted, and a DS whose chain is broken makes the zone bogus, not secure. Only the live sample below splits secure from bogus. The sample's DS share next to the zone's is the representativeness check.
